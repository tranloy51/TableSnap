TableSnap Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This policy explains how the TableSnap Android application (“TableSnap”, “we”, “our” or “the app”) handles information.

Images and text you process

When you select an image, TableSnap uses on-device text recognition to extract text and arrange it into an editable table. The selected image and extracted table content are processed locally on your device. The app does not operate an image-upload service or send your selected images or extracted table contents to a TableSnap developer server.

Files you export

When you export an Excel or CSV file, Android's system document picker lets you choose where to save it. The exported file is saved to the location you select. TableSnap does not upload exported files to a developer-operated server.

Permissions and access

TableSnap uses Android's system photo picker or document selection interface to access the image you choose, and Android's system document picker to save files. The app does not request broad access to your photo library or shared storage.

Google ML Kit diagnostics

TableSnap uses Google's bundled ML Kit text-recognition SDKs to process selected images on your device. The image content and recognized text are processed locally and are not sent to Google or to a TableSnap server. Google states that ML Kit may send limited SDK diagnostics and usage metrics to Google over HTTPS, including device information (such as device model and operating system version), app information (such as package name and app version), per-installation identifiers, feature events, API configuration, input and output sizes, performance metrics and error codes. Google uses these metrics to diagnose, maintain and improve ML Kit and to detect misuse or abuse. Google states that this SDK metrics data is not transferred to third parties. For details, see Google ML Kit Terms and Privacy and Google's ML Kit data disclosure guide.

Accounts, advertising and other services

The current version does not require an account and does not include advertising, in-app purchases, a TableSnap-operated analytics service, or a separate crash-reporting SDK. The ML Kit diagnostics described above are the only SDK telemetry identified for this version.

Children

TableSnap is a general productivity utility and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated service.

Data retention and deletion

TableSnap does not maintain a developer-side database of the images or table contents you process. You control exported files saved on your device or in a storage provider you choose; delete those files using that storage provider's controls.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if app functionality or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when a new policy is published. If a future version adds accounts, cloud processing, advertising, analytics, crash reporting or purchases, this policy and the app's Google Play disclosures will be updated as required.
